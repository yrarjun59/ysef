## Homepage structure observed

- Government/ministry identity, YSEF name and Kupandol-10, Lalitpur address; a search field; language selection; date display; and an important-notice list.
- A separate short notice carousel and an eight-slide image carousel. The image carousel has previous/next controls and slide position; it is not a single static hero image.
- Leadership profile, six service categories, staff/contact information, highlights, recent news/downloads, publications, a photo gallery, and related public links.
- The live site also presents large notice lists and news links, so the redesign gives notices and resources dedicated routes rather than squeezing every item into the hero.

## Second end-to-end reference pass (17 October 2026)

This follow-up pass checked the live homepage, its visible navigation, linked official profiles, information and media pages, and the footer. This is a dated snapshot; current office holders, phone numbers, office hours, notices, and application criteria should be checked at the source before publication.

### Header, utilities, and navigation

- The masthead identifies Government of Nepal, the Ministry of Youth, Labour and Employment, the Youth and Small Entrepreneur Self-Employment Fund (YSEF), and its Kupandol-10, Lalitpur address.
- Utility controls include site search, Nepali/English language choices, Bikram Sambat and Nepal Sambat dates, a waving Nepal flag, and an important-notice ticker with 20 visible items during this capture.
- The homepage has a separate eight-image carousel with Previous and Next controls. The redesign now advances this carousel automatically every 6.5 seconds, pauses on hover or keyboard focus, respects reduced-motion preference, and exposes pause/play, previous/next, and slide-selection controls.
- About links observed: Introduction (`/pages/introduction-3`); Our team (`/our-team/`); Recovery facilitators (`/pages/recovery-facilitator-11`); Organization structure (`/pages/organization-structure`); Citizen charter (`/pages/citizen-charter-12/`); Target and vision (`/pages/target-and-vision-4/`); Operator committee (`/pages/operator-committee/`); and Background (`/pages/background-23/`).
- Services links lead to `/services-list/` and six service categories: insurance premium support, lower interest rate, interest subsidy, product marketing, concessional business loan, and business orientation training.
- Information center links: Self-explanation (`/pages/self-explanation-8`); Annual reports (`/pages/annual-reports-21`); Budgets (`/pages/budgets-10-19`); and Useful information (`/pages/useful-information/`).

The redesign now provides routes and working UI for the core public tasks visible in the reference: Home, grouped navigation, search across services/notices/resources/officials/gallery, English/Nepali toggle, official-site identification explainer, online registration handoff, all six services, notice archive and detail, searchable/filterable resources, about and public accountability information, officials/staff directory, photo/video gallery, contact details and demo feedback form, and footer navigation. Any backend behavior that was not publicly inspectable remains a prototype or a link to the source service; no private government backend was inferred.
